## Features

#### Current:

- Sign up with Google OAuth
- Create a map of your favourite visited restaurants
- Rate your restaurants based on quality and cost
- Categorize your restaurants with custom descriptors

#### Planned:

- Track how frequently you go to your restaurants
- Filter and query your restaurants across many different criteria: cuisine, distance, etc.
- View AI-assisted recommendations of which restaurants to go to
- Share your favourite restaurants with your own friends
- Even better UI/UX improvements
